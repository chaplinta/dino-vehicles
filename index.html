📲 Put Dino Vehicles on your Home Screen
  1. Tap the Share button ⬆️ in Safari's toolbar
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen ➕
  3. Tap Add. Then open it from the new Dino Vehicles icon: full screen, and it works offline.
This only works in Safari. Copy the link and open it in Safari to install.
